Kenyan comfort food

One-pot favourite · 45 minutes

Githeri

Maize and beans simmered until tender, then finished with a rich, gently spiced tomato sauce.

You'll need

  • 3 cups cooked maize and beans
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 potato, diced (optional)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt, pepper and fresh coriander

Bring it together

  1. Soften the onion in oil over medium heat.
  2. Add tomatoes and potato; cook until saucy.
  3. Stir in maize and beans with a splash of water.
  4. Cover and simmer until hot and tender. Season and finish with coriander.
