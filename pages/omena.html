Lake Victoria classic

A lakeside classic · 35 minutes

Omena

Small, flavourful Lake Victoria fish cooked slowly with tomatoes, onion and a bright handful of herbs.

You'll need

  • 2 cups dried omena
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt, pepper and coriander

Bring it together

  1. Rinse omena briefly in warm water and drain well.
  2. Warm the oil and soften the onion; add tomatoes and paste.
  3. Cook until the tomatoes break down, then fold in the omena.
  4. Add a little water and simmer for 10 minutes. Season and garnish with coriander.
