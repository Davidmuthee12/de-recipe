Crisp, golden & moreish

Street-food favourite · 40 minutes

Viazi Karai

Tender potato pieces wrapped in a spiced golden coating and fried until crisp. Best served hot with ukwaju or chilli sauce.

You'll need

  • 4 medium potatoes, peeled and chunked
  • 1 cup gram flour
  • ½ tsp turmeric and ½ tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • Water, salt and oil for frying

Bring it together

  1. Boil potatoes until just tender, then drain and cool.
  2. Whisk gram flour, spices, salt and enough water into a thick batter.
  3. Coat each potato piece in batter.
  4. Fry in hot oil in batches until crisp and golden. Drain and serve with sauce.
