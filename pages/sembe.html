The heart of the table

A Kenyan staple · 25 minutes

Sembe (Ugali)

A firm, comforting maize-meal staple. Serve warm alongside vegetables, stew or your favourite sauce.

You'll need

  • 2 cups maize meal (sembe)
  • 4 cups water, plus a little extra
  • A pinch of salt (optional)

Bring it together

  1. Bring the water to a steady boil in a heavy saucepan.
  2. Stir in a little maize meal to make a light porridge.
  3. Add the remaining meal gradually, stirring firmly to avoid lumps.
  4. Keep turning and pressing until smooth and firm, then shape and serve hot.
